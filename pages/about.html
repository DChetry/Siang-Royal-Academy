Our Foundation

An Academic Institution in Arunachal Pradesh

Siang Royal School is located at Mirbuk Pekung, East Siang, Arunachal Pradesh - 791102, affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE Affiliation: 2230091 | School Code: 35089).

Explore the school's academic environment, infrastructure and publicly available institutional information. Spread across a sprawling 14,136.70 sq. meter campus, the school features 19 standard classrooms of 60 sq. meters each, 6 state-of-the-art laboratories of 56 sq. meters each, and a dedicated team of 23 teaching professionals.
